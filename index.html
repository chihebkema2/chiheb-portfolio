ABOUT ME

Business-minded.
Technology-driven.

An entrepreneurial path shaped by international marketplace experience, cross-border sourcing and a commitment to building useful digital products.

I'm Chiheb Eddine Kemaoun, an Algeria-based entrepreneur active in international e-commerce, brand development and AI-driven software projects.

Through hands-on work with Amazon's U.S. and UAE marketplaces, I've gained practical experience in product research, supplier communication, listing management, FBA logistics and advertising. I value the operational details that turn an idea into a functioning business.

I'm also the founder of Lexiforme Architect, an AI-powered document-processing platform in development, and the owner of the FRINGAL brand project, associated with a U.S. trademark application.

My focus is to create independent businesses, build trustworthy brands and develop tools that solve real problems.
